# Secondwind

**Reparieren statt wegwerfen. Mit Recht.** · *Your stuff deserves a second wind.*

Secondwind is a free, mobile-first helper (DE/EN) for using the EU Right to Repair in Germany (Directive (EU) 2024/1799, in force in Germany since 31 July 2026).

## What v1 does
1. **Rechte prüfen / Check my rights:** tells you whether the seller's legal warranty (§§ 437, 439 BGB) or the manufacturer's repair obligation (§ 479b BGB) applies to your device.
2. **Brief erstellen / Write the letter:** generates a ready-to-send German letter to the seller or the manufacturer, with an explanation in English or German.
3. **Wo reparieren? / Where to repair:** links to repair cafés, iFixit and the European Consumer Centre (EVZ), plus the status of the regional repair bonus schemes (Reparaturbonus).

The whole app is a single `index.html` with no backend. It stores and sends no data.

## Disclaimer
Secondwind gives general information, not legal advice. The legal status is as of 23 Nov 2026. Sources are listed in the page footer.
